## 🌌 The Spark

Every operating system starts as an idea. **AgneaX OS** started as a flame.

Born from the Sanskrit word *Agni* (fire), AgneaX is an open-source operating system forged from the ground up for people who are tired of choosing between *beautiful* and *fast*. It wraps a hand-tailored **AgneaX Desktop Environment** in the declarative **AgniUI Framework**, so every window glows with frosted glass, every click ripples with micro-animations, and every boot feels like switching on something brand new.

Developer, creator, or just someone who wants a computer that feels *good*: pull up a chair. The fire's already lit.

## ⚡ Highlights

- **A Desktop With a Soul**: Frosted glass blur, cyber-cyan highlights that adapt to your mood, deep titanium-dark backdrops, and shadows that give every surface depth.
- **The Living Status Panel**: Dynamic status capsules for Volume, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Hotspot, Bluetooth, DND, and Battery at a single glance.
- **Dedicated Wi-Fi Hotspot**: Turn your PC or laptop into a Wi-Fi hotspot in a single click with custom SSID and secure WPA2 protection.
- **Fluid & Fast**: Engineered to consume minimal system resources while maintaining ultra-smooth 60 FPS desktop compositor animations.
- **Creative & Utility App Suite**: Studio webcam recording (`agneax-camera`), GPU shader photo browser (`agneax-imageviewer`), accelerated terminal (`agneax-terminal`), and double-click package management (`agneax-debinstaller`).
- **Dual Boot, Without the Drama**: Calamares installer safely resizes existing Windows partitions without data loss and auto-configures your high-resolution GRUB boot menu.

## 📥 Getting Started

1. Download the latest bootable ISO from [**AgneaX OS Releases**](https://github.com/agneax/AgneaxOS/releases/latest).
2. Flash the image onto a USB flash drive (minimum 4 GB) using [Rufus](https://rufus.ie), [Ventoy](https://www.ventoy.net), or `dd`.
3. Plug in the USB and boot your PC.
4. Choose **"Start AgneaX OS"** to test live, or launch the guided installer to set up standalone or dual-boot alongside Windows.
